What this app is

Go Taxation Suite is an assistance tool for saving receipts and income/expense entries and for preparing working papers. Figures are indicative. They may be incomplete.

  • It is not financial, tax or legal advice.
  • It is not an official ATO product and does not lodge a BAS or income-tax return.
  • The BAS screen is an ATO-box worksheet (G1, 1A, G11, 1B, 9) you can download and copy when you lodge on the ATO Business Portal or with a registered agent.
  • EOFY reports and forecasts are live working papers, not lodged returns.

You remain responsible for checking amounts and lodging with the ATO or a registered tax agent.

Accounts

You must be signed in to upload or change records. Keep your password private. You can delete your own account under Profile → Delete account (type DELETE plus your password). That permanently removes the login and stored records for this product.
