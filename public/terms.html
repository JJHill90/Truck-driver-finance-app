What these apps are

Driver Hub (Taxation Hub / Fuel Hub) and Go Taxation Suite are assistance tools for saving receipts and income/expense entries and for preparing working papers. Figures are indicative. They may be incomplete.

  • They are not financial, tax or legal advice.
  • They are not an official ATO product and do not lodge a BAS or income-tax return.
  • The BAS screen is an ATO-box worksheet (G1, 1A, G11, 1B, 9) you can download and copy when you lodge on the ATO Business Portal or with a registered agent.
  • EOFY reports and forecasts are live working papers, not lodged returns.

You remain responsible for checking amounts and lodging with the ATO or a registered tax agent.

Accounts

You must be signed in to upload or change records. Keep your password private. You can delete your own account under Profile → Delete account (type DELETE plus your password). That permanently removes the login and stored records for that product’s disk.

Subscriptions (website Stripe — not store IAP)

Paid plans are billed on the website by Stripe. The native App Store / Play shells are login-and-record apps in this version: they do not sell Apple or Google in-app purchases, so there is no store “Restore purchases” receipt. Open the website Profile → Plan to subscribe, cancel, or manage a card.
