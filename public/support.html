Support

Last updated 24 September 2026. For the website and the Play / App Store app.

Email support@godriverhub.com. In the app, open Support in the sidebar and send a message — we reply to the address you enter.

Common requests

  • Account or password. Use Forgot username / password on the login screen, or email us.
  • Delete my data. Profile → Delete account (type DELETE and your password), or email us. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Billing. Paid plans are on the website via Stripe (Profile → Plan). The store app does not sell in-app purchases.
  • Receipts and tax figures. This is an assistance tool, not tax advice. You (or your agent) lodge with the ATO.
