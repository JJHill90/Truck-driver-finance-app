Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026. Applies to Driver Hub (Taxation Hub / Fuel Hub) and Go Taxation Suite, including the websites and native app shells.

These products help you record receipts, income and expenses and prepare working papers (including an ATO-box BAS worksheet). They are not tax advice, not an official ATO form, and do not lodge a BAS or tax return for you.

Who we are

Driver Hub and Go Taxation Suite are operated as separate hosted products from the same codebase. Contact: support@godriverhub.com.

  • Driver Hub — truck-driver tax records (Taxation Hub) and diesel planning (Fuel Hub).
  • Go Taxation Suite — general Australian tax records (PAYG, sole trader, partnership).

What we collect

  • Account — username, email, salted password hash, plan/billing identifiers.
  • Profile — name, employer or occupation, salary band, work vehicle / entity type, optional presets.
  • Ledgers and uploads — income and expense rows, receipt/payslip images or PDFs, OCR text extracted from those files.
  • Device — session cookie after sign-in; camera or photo-library access only when you scan or pick a receipt.
  • Support — messages you send through the Support form.
  • Payments (website only) — Stripe customer and subscription IDs. Card numbers are handled by Stripe, not stored in our database.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use receipt photos for advertising.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only to photograph or upload receipts and payslips that you choose to save to your account. Android and iOS will show a permission prompt the first time you scan. You can refuse and still upload a file instead. Photos stay in your signed-in account (and encrypted backups of that account store) until you delete the receipt or delete the account.

How we use the information

  • Sign you in and keep your records private to your profile.
  • Run on-device or server OCR so you can approve totals and categories.
  • Show estimates, forecasts and ATO-box worksheets from your saved figures.
  • Apply upload quotas and paid-plan entitlements.
  • Send optional recovery or support email when mail is configured.

Cookies and the native apps

Sign-in uses an HttpOnly session cookie. On the website it is SameSite=Lax. In the Capacitor Play / App Store WebView (cross-origin), the cookie is SameSite=None; Secure so the session works. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

Records stay until you delete a row, a receipt, or the whole account. Hosted backups (typically about five daily archives) may retain a copy until those archives rotate.

You can delete your own account in the app: Profile → Delete account. Type DELETE and your password. That wipes the login, ledgers, receipt files and (when Stripe is configured) cancels any paid subscription immediately so you are not billed after deletion. The primary moderator account cannot be self-deleted.

You can also email support@godriverhub.com and ask us to delete the profile.

Payments

Paid plans are sold on the website through Stripe Checkout and the Stripe Customer Portal. The native apps do not take Apple or Google in-app purchases in this version. See the Terms for price, auto-renewal and cancel paths.

Hosting and international transfers

Application data is stored on the product’s hosted disk (Render). Email, if enabled, is sent through SMTP or Resend. Stripe processes payments. Those processors may store data outside Australia under their own terms.

Children

The apps are for adult taxpayers and business users. They are not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page when the products change. The “Last updated” date at the top is the current version.
