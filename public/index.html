Data backups

Full copies of accounts, ledgers, receipt files and history. The server archives daily at 5:00 pm Australia/Sydney. Keep this Profile tab open around then and today’s backup downloads to this device automatically (you can also Download any time). Restore overwrites live data (a safety backup is taken first).
