Recommended best way to scan your receipts
  • Whole receipt in frame — try to include the top of the receipt and fit the date in the photo.
  • Flat and sharp — dark surface, even light, no finger glare over the total or ABN.
  • Scanner needs — vendor name, ABN, SALE TOTAL (or amount due), and the purchase date.

Primary mod — user profiles

Create profiles for drivers who request access. Open any profile to assist them: reset login details, override mistaken ledger/profile edits, and restore earlier data snapshots. The primary mod account cannot be deleted.

Data backups

Full copies of accounts, ledgers, receipt files and history. The server archives daily at 5:00 pm Australia/Sydney. Keep this Profile tab open around then and today’s backup downloads to this device automatically (you can also Download any time). Restore overwrites live data (a safety backup is taken first).

Fuel class & tank

These class codes are finer than a general heavy rigid. Add the vehicle on this profile (registration + class such as XN93DX, YN16BQ or YN17BQ) and mark it Active — Fuel Hub uses that tank for travel range and fill routes. You can type a custom code and tank litres to monitor. This is separate from ATO work cars on Car Expenses.

YN16BQ is a standard heavy-rigid tank. Compact (XN93DX) fills more often; long-range (YN17BQ) carries more diesel mass.
