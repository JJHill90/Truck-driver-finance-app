About us

Go Taxation Suite is an independent Australian tax-records app for PAYG employees, sole traders and partnerships — any occupation. Photograph receipts, save income, and keep a live EOFY working paper you can take to your accountant.

New profiles start on Free (15 uploads a month and one on-screen report). Pro and Pro+ are paid on this website with Stripe — not as in-app purchases on Google Play or the App Store. The same login works in the browser and in the Android / iOS apps.

We are not the ATO and we do not provide tax, financial or legal advice. The app does not lodge a BAS or tax return. You (or your registered agent) lodge with the ATO.
